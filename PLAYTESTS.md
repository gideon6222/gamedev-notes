## Coreward — github.com/gideon6222/coreward

Live at https://gideon6222.github.io/coreward/

Dig toward a planet core, sell ore at the surface pad, buy upgrades, break the core and the
planet explodes, launch to a harder planet. Seven upgrade lines. Fuel and hull heat are the
two pressures pushing you back up.

### 2026-09-06 — first run
"the game looks like it runs great"

Confirmed working on the S26 Ultra in Chrome and installed as a PWA. No performance or
rendering complaints. No feedback yet on balance, pacing or fun, only that it runs.

Open questions to ask him about next time:
- Does the opening feel too slow before the first upgrades land?
- Does the heat below 60m arrive before a Cooling Rig is affordable?
- Is the trip back to the surface tedious or a good breather?
- Did reaching a core feel like a payoff worth the climb?
